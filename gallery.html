Winston Mojalefa Thekiso

Ballpoint Pen Artist | Graphic Designer | Mbombela, Mpumalanga

Winston Mojalefa Thekiso holds a diploma in Graphic Design from Technikon Witwatersrand. His work interrogates South African social, political, and economic landscapes — merging portraiture with geometric abstraction and floral motifs. Each piece invites the viewer to deconstruct stereotypes and construct their own meaning.

Working exclusively in ballpoint pen, Thekiso's practice spans human psychology, philosophical inquiry, and visual storytelling. His pieces are held in private collections across Johannesburg, Cape Town, and internationally.
