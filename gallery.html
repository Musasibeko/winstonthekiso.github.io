Winston Motalefa Thekiso

Ballpoint Pen Artist | Graphic Designer | Mbombela, Mpumalanga

Winston Motalefa Thekiso holds a diploma in Graphic Design from Technikon Witwatersrand. His work interrogates South African social, political, and economic landscapes — merging portraiture with geometric abstraction and floral motifs.

Working exclusively in ballpoint pen, Thekiso's practice spans human psychology, philosophical inquiry, and visual storytelling.
